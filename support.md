# fitHer Support

Need help with fitHer? Email **neosdevshelp@gmail.com** and we'll reply as soon as we can.

### Common questions

**How is my calorie target calculated?**
Your maintenance calories are your body weight in kg × 30. The app subtracts a safe daily deficit based on how much you want to lose and in how many days, and splits it between food and exercise.

**How do I change my goal or timeline?**
On the Today screen, tap your plan card, or go to Settings → Goal & timeline.

**How do I change my diet, cuisine or workout preferences?**
Settings → Diet & workout preferences.

**How do I cancel fitHer Premium?**
iPhone Settings → your name → Subscriptions → fitHer → Cancel Subscription.

**I bought Premium but it isn't showing.**
Settings → Restore purchases, while signed in with the same Apple ID.

**How do I delete my data?**
Settings → Delete all my data. Your data is only stored on your iPhone.
