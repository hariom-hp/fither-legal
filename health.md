# Health Advisory

**fitHer** · Last updated: November 11, 2026

### 1. General wellness only

fitHer gives general nutrition and exercise guidance for healthy adults. It is not a medical device and cannot diagnose, treat, prevent or cure any disease.

### 2. Talk to your doctor first if you

- Are pregnant, trying to get pregnant or breastfeeding.
- Have or have had an eating disorder.
- Have heart disease, high blood pressure, diabetes, kidney or thyroid disease.
- Have a joint, bone or back injury.
- Take medicine that affects your appetite, weight or heart rate.

### 3. Safety limits built into fitHer

- Food target never goes below 1,200 kcal a day for women (1,500 for men).
- Weight loss is limited to about 1% of your body weight per week, and never more than a 1,000 kcal daily deficit. If you ask for a faster timeline, the plan is stretched to a safe one.
- The app will not plan weight loss below a healthy weight for your height (BMI 18.5).
- No plans for anyone under 18.

### 4. How the numbers are worked out

- Maintenance calories = your body weight in kg × 30 (you can switch to the Mifflin-St Jeor equation in Settings).
- These are estimates. Your real needs may be higher or lower.

### 5. Stop and get help

If you feel chest pain or pressure, an irregular heartbeat, sudden shortness of breath, dizziness or fainting while exercising, stop right away and call your local emergency number.
