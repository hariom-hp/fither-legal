# Privacy Policy

**fitHer** · Last updated: November 11, 2026

This policy explains what fitHer ("the app", "we") does with your information. In short: you don't need an account, everything you enter stays on your iPhone, and we never see it.

### 1. What the app stores

To build your calorie target and your day-by-day diet and workout plan, the app stores what you enter:

- Profile: age, height, current weight and goal weight.
- Goal: how much you want to lose and in how many days.
- Preferences: activity level, workout days, experience, focus areas, cuisine, diet type, allergies, foods to avoid, meals per day, wake-up and bed times.
- Logs: meals you mark as eaten, foods you add, water, workouts and weigh-ins.

### 2. Where it is stored

- All of this is saved only on your iPhone, inside the app's private storage.
- If you use iCloud Backup or back up your iPhone to a computer, this data is included in that backup. Apple handles those backups under Apple's privacy policy.
- We do not run any servers. The app has no account system and does not send your data to us or to anyone else.

### 3. Camera and photos

- The app asks for the camera or your photo library only when you choose to scan a meal.
- The photo is analysed on your iPhone with Apple's built-in Vision framework to estimate calories. It is not saved by the app and is never uploaded.

### 4. Notifications

- If you turn on the weekly weigh-in reminder, the app asks for permission to send notifications.
- The reminder is scheduled on your iPhone. No data is sent anywhere to deliver it.

### 5. Purchases

- fitHer Premium is sold through Apple's App Store. Apple processes the payment.
- We never receive your name, card details or billing address.
- The app asks Apple's StoreKit whether you have an active subscription. This check happens between your iPhone and Apple.

### 6. What we don't do

- No advertising, no ad networks and no tracking across apps or websites.
- No analytics or third-party SDKs that collect your data.
- We never sell, rent or share your information. Your health and fitness data is never used for marketing.

### 7. Health information

The weight, food and workout data you enter is used only to calculate your plan inside the app. fitHer is not a medical service. See the Health Advisory in the app.

### 8. Children

fitHer is for adults aged 18 and over. The app does not create a weight-loss plan for anyone who enters an age under 18.

### 9. Your choices

- You can change your details at any time in Settings.
- To delete everything, go to Settings → Delete all my data. Deleting the app also removes all its data from your iPhone.
- Because we don't collect or hold any of your data, there is nothing for us to export or delete on our side. You can still contact us with any privacy question.

### 10. Changes

If this policy changes, we will update the date at the top and the new version will appear in the app and at https://hariom-hp.github.io/fither-legal/privacy.
